Photography

Whether it's a graduation, a funeral, a wedding, a christening or anything else, I document your important moments so you can focus on the moment itself and relive it through the photos afterwards.

My strength is documentary photography, capturing moments as authentically as possible. A shoot usually produces between about a hundred and a thousand frames, from which I select and edit the best ones for you.

My aim is to offer more affordable, customer-friendly photography without compromising on quality or creativity.
Alongside photography I also offer video, such as a wedding video or a memorial video of the occasion.

Frequently asked questions

How are the photos delivered?

I deliver the photos digitally via a cloud service, where you can download them to your device. As a separate extra, you can buy a memory card with the photos delivered to you.

Good to know

We often take group photos too: one with all the guests, plus separate photos of smaller groups.

Booking fee?

Once we've agreed on the date, I charge a booking fee, which is deducted from the final invoice.
