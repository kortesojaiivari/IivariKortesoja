Video production

In general

There are no fixed price packages, because every production is different. The price is always agreed per project.

I put together a crew to match the size of the production. A crew can have 2–10 people to achieve the result you want.

Your production is divided into three phases

1

Pre-production

The video is planned as completely as possible before filming, so the schedule stays on track.

Pre-production defines, for example, the premise of the video, the target audience, the structure and the storyboard.

2

Production (filming)

A crew is assembled for the project and the equipment for the production is planned.

The video is then filmed according to the plan.

3

Post-production

Post-production covers editing, colour grading, sound design and any effects or 3D animation.

You also get B-roll footage to use freely in the future.

AIAISI-assisted translation

Take your production to global distribution. With translations localised for each country, your content reaches viewers around the world even if they don't understand the original language.

  • Subtitle translation for worldwide distribution
  • Country-specific versions of titles and thumbnails

Workflow

1

Subtitles

Subtitles are created for the programme, or existing ones are used.

2

Adding context

The subtitles are prepared for Super Intelligence by adding hidden context, so the translation reads naturally.

3

Manual review rounds

To make the translation natural in other languages too, I have the master translation for the bulk translation reviewed several times and correct it by hand. This makes the bulk translation as natural as possible.

4

Bulk translation

I use Google's open, locally run TranslateGemma model to bulk-translate the subtitles into every language.

5

Review

The translations are checked with a paid Super Intelligence model by comparing the original human-made reference translation with the machine translation.

6

Thumbnails

For YouTube, I design the thumbnail layout and generate country-specific thumbnail versions in bulk with a paid image generation model via an API.

7

Delivery

Depending on the platform, for example YouTube, I add the translations and the thumbnail and title versions for each language by hand.

This is my current workflow. Super Intelligence is developing fast, so the process keeps getting easier and faster over time.
